Battle.net (the community), Discord, Telegram and GitHub icons for the «Помощь» tab: the logos of [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons) (CC0 1.0), in the brands' colours (GitHub in white for the dark window), rendered at 32×32 into TGA. The logos are trademarks of their owners; they only mark the links to those sites.

`chat.tga` — the addon's icon for the chat, drawn by `tools/make_icon.py`: the speech bubble and waves alone on a clear background (the framed `Icon.tga` is an opaque square there, the chat's background being clear), before «Barrens Voice:» with `|T…|t`.

`smile.tga` — a smiley drawn by `tools/make_icon.py` (the game's fonts have no emoji), shown inline in the «Помощь» text with `|T…|t`.
