# Changelog

## 0.3.0 (в работе)

- Retail WoW: the companion works with one chosen game — `WowChatVoice-Retail.cmd` (or `"game": "retail"`) starts it for retail: retail's folder, Battle.net's «WoW», a voice bank of its own (`voices-retail`). Voice files retail has not downloaded yet are taken from an installed WoW Forever: the same content keys, the same files. The addon's TOC lists both games' interface versions.
- Retail's races speak with their own voices from the game's Russian voice-over (the character's voice, three variants per sex): blood elves, draenei, goblins, worgen (and in human form), pandaren, dracthyr (and in dragon form), the allied races, earthen and haranir. A race whose recordings the client lacks speaks as its kin. Forever's bank keeps only Forever's races.
- Retail closes chat to addons in dungeons, raids, boss fights, Mythic+ and PvP (text, sender and GUID come as secret values): such lines are not read and no error is raised; the addon says so once, and the hidden chat window shows them.
- The chat waits for Wayfarer (WayfarerRU) as for CatQuest: its quests, NPC lines, place lore and radio stories pause the chat, a paused Wayfarer does not. One setting for both: «Ждать озвучку квестов (CatQuest, Wayfarer)».
- «ё» where the chat typed «е» and only «ё» can be meant («ждем» → «ждём», «еще» → «ещё», «утес» → «утёс»): the safe dictionary of eyo-kernel (MIT, about 107 thousand word forms); «все»/«всё» and the like stay as typed. `restore_yo` in config.json turns it off. RUAccent never put «ё» itself.
- The dictionary reads «чиловая» as «чил+овая» in every form, «пиши» as «пиш+и», «губа» as «губ+а», «тикет» as «т+икет» in every form, «хелло» as «хелл+оу», «менеджмент» and «манаменеджмент» with the stress on «м+е» in every form, «утес» as «утёс» in every form, and «ждем», «ждет», «ждешь», «ждете» with ё.
- «РЛ» is read as «руины Лордерона» (declined after a preposition: «у руин Лордерона»), and as «рейд-лидер» where the words around it say so («нужен РЛ», «РЛ сказал», «пишите РЛу», «с РЛом»). Declension: a hyphenated word declines as one («с рейд-лидером»), and «руины» as a thing («в руины», not «в руин»).

## 0.2.1

- Where the strip is can be set: «Мост…» in the settings (the strip is called the bridge there, with a tooltip on what it is; the program is the companion app there, «приложение-компаньон», or just «компаньон») — the top edge (as before), the bottom edge, or a place of your own (X, Y and width in screen pixels; the fields start from the top or bottom line's numbers). Another addon drawing in the same place — such as wow-ai, which draws its own strip in the top left corner — hid ours, and chat lines were lost while it was up. While the window is open, an orange frame shows where the strip will go; «Сохранить» moves it.
- The strip is drawn above the rest of the game's interface: it belonged to the game world's frame, and everything in the interface — the minimap at the top right, the action bars at the bottom — was drawn over it whatever its layer. It still stays up when the interface is hidden (Alt+Z).
- The strip is as thick as it needs to be for the longest line: a narrow place of your own gets more rows (220 pixels wide — 7 rows, 14 pixels), and the bridge window shows the thickness. On a 1280-pixel-wide screen it now has two rows: one held 469 bytes, and parts of your dictionary edits (480 bytes) came to the program cut.
- The program learns where the strip is from the addon, which tells it before moving the strip, on the strip where it still is; no chat line is lost while it moves. If the program misses that, or is started while the game runs, it reads the place from the addon's saved settings, and failing that finds the strip anywhere in the game window within seconds.
- The dictionary reads «кв» as «квест», and a number before «шт» as so many pieces in the right form: «6шт» — «шесть штук», «1 шт.» — «одна штука».
- A quest's number that quest addons put after its name in chat («[[25] Свиток с рунами (3513)]») is no longer read out.
- The chat windows can stay out of sight («Прятать окно чата, пока на нём нет мыши», off by default): the chat is read aloud anyway, so the windows — text, background, tabs, edit box — are transparent until the mouse is over them or you type. A line the program does not read aloud (a system message, loot, a channel switched off, your own line, an addon's message) shows its window for 10 seconds; only lines in the tab on screen do (crafting nearby in a background combat log tab no longer flashes the chat). The mouse over the chat counts only when no other window lies over it there.
- At login the chat says whether the reading works: «озвучка чата работает», or that the companion app is not running and the chat is read as soon as it is started. The companion notes the time in a file in the addon's folder every 15 seconds, and the game reads it at login and /reload (not during play); the settings show it too.
- A welcome on the first run: what the addon is, that the companion app does the reading, where the settings are. After an update, one line on what is new.
- The race voices option no longer says they come from CatQuest: since 0.2.0 they come from the game's own Russian voice-over.
- `/wcv last` no longer loses lines: a replay of more than 8 lines went past the companion's queue, and the extra ones were dropped (on a 14-line replay, 6 were); over 20 lines, the strip's queue dropped the first ones too. Replayed lines are now never dropped and do not count toward the queue's 8 live lines, and a line already made into sound waits for its turn instead of being thrown away.
- Lines other players' addons write into the chat for them — Questie's party progress («{звезда} Questie: … 1/1»), DBM, BigWigs, Details! and others — are no longer read («Не читать сообщения аддонов», on by default): with Questie on two players in a group each progress line was read twice.
- `/wcv last` remembers the last 50 lines across /reload and a game restart: they are kept in the addon's saved settings.
- The dictionary reads «найдется» (and «найдет», «найдешь», «найдем», «найдете») with «ё», and «еще» as «ещё».
- The companion keeps every model in one Hugging Face cache (`hf_home`, by default the `hf` folder next to it): Whisper, used to build the voice bank, lay in the system's cache apart from F5 and Vocos.
- Warnings and errors the libraries write to stderr (torch, transformers) go into the companion's log file too, marked `[stderr]`.
- The dictionary reads «тг» as «телеграм», «ага» with the stress on the last «а», and «доведёте» (and «доведёт», «доведёшь», «доведём») with «ё».
- A line that is nothing but questions is announced «Громмаш спрашивает: …» instead of «пишет» (the voices raise their tone at a question mark unevenly, the verb tells it by ear); yells and whispers keep their verbs.
- The dictionary reads «Серёжа» in every case, and «учёный» in its forms, with «ё».
- «1 место» is read as a count, «одно место» (it was «первое»): in chat it is mostly «1 место в группу»; «15 место» and «на 2 месте» stay ordinal.
- The dictionary reads «Серёжа» in every case, and «учёный» in its forms, with «ё».
- Under «Общие каналы» each numbered channel has its own box: General, Trade, Looking for group, Defense and the others — to read General and not Trade, say. All are on by default, so nothing changes until you switch one off.
- With the chat windows hidden, a whisper or a guild line can show the window though it is read aloud («Показывать при шёпоте», «Показывать при строке гильдии», off by default).
- Speed and volume: the sliders' 100 % is now the default sound (it used to be 125 % speed and 120 % volume, which sounded better); the speed goes 40–160 % and the volume 0–200 %, so both marks stand in the middle at 100. Saved settings are converted once, so everything sounds as before.
- `/wcv reset` is gone: handing the race voices out again is the «Перераздать голоса» button, which asks first; a command for a thing done once in a blue moon is one more to remember.
- The settings window is in the game's own tabs now — «Общее», «Каналы и чтение», «Голоса», «Словарь» — instead of one long window and three more behind buttons. Each tab starts with a line on what is on it, and the window is as high as the tab needs. Speed and volume are on the first tab; the bridge settings are its foldable section; the Windows voices list and the dictionary are tabs of their own. The «Общие каналы» box holds its channels under it, as «Озвучивать имена» does. «Склонять замены после предлогов» and «Только то, что видно в окнах чата» are always on and no longer settings. Channel labels carry this client's own chat commands («Группа (/гр)»), and the settings say plainly whether the companion was running at login.
- The companion stops at once, saying where it looked and what to do, when the addon is not installed — instead of launching the game and reading nothing.
- Skyborne (небеснорождённые), Forever's own race, speak with their own voices from the game's Russian voice-over — the character's voice, three variants per sex — instead of human ones. The voice bank is rebuilt once, a few minutes before the next game start.

## 0.2.0

- Race voices now come from the game client itself: the Russian voice-over of the playable races — each race and sex has the player character's voice and three NPC voice types (orc guard, shady and standard; human official, standard and warrior; …), each also in a lower and a higher copy, 12 voices per race and sex. Real actors instead of synthesized CatQuest voices: the orc sounds low and rough and the speech comes out clearer. CatQuest is no longer needed.
- The client's files are only read, never changed, and the game is not touched: the voice bank is made on your machine before the game starts, the first time and after each game update (a few minutes: Whisper hears the lines for their text), and kept until the next update.
- Slow voices speak faster: the game's recordings differ up to fourfold in tempo (a hurried error message of a character, a solemn NPC greeting), and a voice slower than the faster quarter of the voices is now drawn most of the way toward it (by at most 1.8 times; the Tauren elder went from 18.5 to 12.3 seconds on a test line); faster voices stay as they are. Each character also speaks at a tempo of their own (±8 %, from the name), so two characters given the same voice still sound like two people.
- Endings no longer get cut off: when a line's speech runs to the very end of the length given to it (one line in ten), it is made again with a quarter more length.
- The race voices load while the game starts instead of waiting for its first chat line (unless the addon was last set to Windows voices).
- Very high and very low voices no longer rasp in questions: the pitch analysis behind the question intonation had a fixed 60–500 Hz range, and gnome women speak at 450–500 Hz, the orc character at 55–70 Hz; outside the range it lost the voice's periods. It now follows the voice.
- Numbers get their stress like other words ("шестн+адцать", not "ш+естнадцать"): they are turned into words before the stress marks are put, not after.
- A line with a word the stress model splits its own way ("RU/UA", "<MIR>") no longer loses the stress marks of all its words ("игр+оков" for "игрок+ов"): only that word goes without.
- The dictionary reads Discord as «диск+орд», the Crossroads as «перекр+ёсток» and greaves as «п+оножи» in every case, «ПС» as «Пещеры стенаний», «че» as «чё», «серега» as «серёга», «пикабу» as «пикаб+у», PvE as «пэ-вэ-йе».
- A dictionary stem (an entry with `*`) keeps the case typed in chat: «на перекрёстке» was turned into «на перекрёсток», as the declension after a preposition is meant for abbreviations («в ОП») that have no ending of their own.
- Several lines in a row from one character in one channel are read as one speech: the name only before the first, when the next line comes while the previous one is still being read or within 5 seconds after it ends (someone else in between — the name again).
- The dictionary is applied by the program now: it reads the default dictionary straight from the addon's files and picks up a change within seconds, with no /reload; your edits from the dictionary window come to it as before. A character's name is no longer run through the dictionary (a character named «Лок» stays «Лок»).
- Chat lines dropped after waiting too long for CatQuest are noted in the program's log.
- A chat line dropped because the queue is full (more than 8 lines waiting in a busy chat) is noted in the program's log; before, it vanished without a trace.
- `/wcv debug`: the addon notes in the program's log each chat line it does not send, with the reason (chat type off, not shown in any chat window, hidden by another addon's chat filter, your own), and when CatQuest starts and stops speaking — the log has the time the game's chat lacks.
- The chat no longer waits for CatQuest when CatQuest reports a track still playing well past the track's own length: its «playing» gets stuck at times, and chat lines were held and then dropped while nothing was heard.
- A number is read as an ordinal where only an ordinal fits: «с 15 уровня» — «с пятнадцатого уровня», «на 2 этаже» — «на втором этаже», «15 уровень» — «пятнадцатый уровень». With words that number rather than count (level, floor, place, rank, phase, season, chapter, act, wave, slot, boss) also «с 21 уровня» — «с двадцать первого» and «хил 15 уровня» — «пятнадцатого». Counts stay counts: «200 опыта», «на 2 уровня выше», «5 раз».
- «Число» as a date is a word that numbers: «до 11 числа» — «до одиннадцатого ч+исла». After an ordinal, «числа» and «места» get the singular's stress: the stress model always took them for the plural («числ+а», «мест+а»).
- The dictionary reads «аук» in every case as «аукцион» («на ауке» — «на аукционе»), and the Ragefire Chasm typed in full as «Огненная пр+опасть», not with the verb's stress «пропа́сть», in the case its preposition asks for («в Огненная Пропасть» — «в Огненную пропасть»).
- The dictionary reads «танчить» as «т+анчить» in every form, and «МСК» as «эм-эс-к+а».
- The chat no longer talks over CatQuest: while CatQuest reads a quest, chat lines wait and are read once it is done (lines that waited over 40 seconds are skipped); a setting, on by default. The quest is not paused for the chat instead: the game cannot resume a sound in the middle, so the quest would start over after every chat line.
- When CatQuest starts speaking while a chat line is being read, the chat stops at once and waits; once CatQuest is silent, the line it cut is read again from its start, then the rest. A pause whose end never reaches the program ends by itself after 90 seconds.
- Quest and item links read as their names: no brackets, no quest level tag ("[24]"), and the word before a link no longer runs into it.
- Item links coloured by quality name ("|cnIQ1:", as newer clients send them) no longer leave the colour code in the speech, and two links in a row are read as two names. A colon right before a Russian letter («1:Сильный», «внимание:Привет») no longer garbles the letter: the rule for the «:Р» smiley took the Russian «Р» apart into bytes and ate the first byte of the letter.
- Words in capitals («РАЗЫСКИВАЕТСЯ», «ВСЁ», «СТОЙ») are read as words, not as abbreviations with the stress in the wrong place; an abbreviation the dictionary does not know is read letter by letter when it cannot be said as syllables, as in Russian speech: «ППС» — «пэ-пэ-эс», «ЦРУ», «США», and a syllable it begins with is said as one: «ГИБДД» — «ги-бэ-дэ-дэ»; «ОМОН» or «ВУЗ» are read as words.
- Swear words hidden from the chat filter are read as the words they are: a bracket pair for "х" ("на)(уя"; it used to become a full stop), Latin look-alike letters ("xyйня"), a digit inside a word ("п0шел") and asterisks for letters ("бл*ть").
- `assignments.json` (which voice each character has) no longer grows without end on a busy server: characters not heard for 60 days are forgotten (one who comes back gets a voice again, the same one when there are voices to go round), and the file is written every 30 seconds and on exit instead of on every chat line.
- Races without voices of their own in the client speak as their kin: goblins and vulpera as gnomes, blood elves as night elves, and so on. If you pinned a race voice to a player in `players`, the voice names changed (`orc-male/standard` and the like).
